# Refine Harness Audit

Audit the evaluator or workflow role, not the document in its place. Establish the declared task purpose for each assessed axis before treating a historical judgment as correct or wrong. The current user purpose is the evaluation target; historical Cards, prompts, and Skills explain what the role was told, not whether that instruction satisfies the current purpose. A role may follow its old instruction yet need adaptation. Keep those two conclusions separate.

## Expert boundaries

Anchor each diagnosis in both the original trace and the current boundary table. The trace establishes what happened; the table establishes how to evaluate it and at which level. Explicit user constraints remain superior to the generated interpretation. Establish the applicable comparison unit and scope before deciding whether the observed behavior is a problem.

Explain the actual evidence and the applicable table rule together, including counterevidence that limits the diagnosis. A semantic reference to the rule is sufficient; no fixed reporting fields are required. If the table is insufficient, conflicting, or does not define how its criteria apply at the observed level, identify that gap and the affected judgment. Preserve the resulting uncertainty rather than filling it with a habitual default or forcing a finding.

For each task, distinguish the content comparison target, style requirements, and authoritative source material. Use explicit user purposes and actual requirements; do not silently replace an undefined axis with factual identity, topic identity, or a preferred writing template. A comparison Gold is not automatically authoritative source material. An undefined boundary can be a finding when the trace shows which judgment depends on it; uncertainty alone does not justify inventing a true/false answer.

Keep responsibilities separate. The Extractor represents the source; the Matcher selects comparable concepts; the Aligner assesses the declared content or style relation. A permissible content difference must not become a style penalty or a matching exclusion merely to recover a stricter criterion. Check the specific role's visible operation before attributing an error to it or another phase.

Distinguish a full Aspect description, its quoted evidence, the original passage, and a previous role's rationale. State which layer supports the finding and what the other layers contribute as counterevidence. Multiple related sentences may supply missing context, but their availability alone proves neither adequate coverage nor a correct decision. Do not infer an internal cause from a public rationale or call a claim invented when another supplied layer supports it.

## Findings before proposed changes

Identify whether the evidence supports a missing or ambiguous boundary, a criterion conflicting with the task purpose, or execution departing from an applicable criterion. Describe the actual event, the relevant task axis, its effect, and the strongest counterevidence. If the relation is unresolved, name the affected judgment and missing authority rather than prescribing a unique outcome. A score change, repeated target choice, or existing rule is not by itself proof of a defect or of success.

Assess whether the constraint exists, the operation occurred, and the intended result was achieved as separate questions. A finding may remain valid without a new modification. Only propose a change when its operation differs meaningfully from the current configuration and has an observable validation condition; do not substitute reminders to be more careful for a method. Repeated instances may support one mechanism, but retain their event references and do not generalize an isolated event to the entire role.

Report supported findings without requiring a nonempty list. Keep configuration adaptation distinct from an accusation that historical execution violated its own instructions. Preserve task, phase, input version, and source attribution. Use only public evidence; do not request private reasoning. Do not rewrite the Expert, document, scoring formula, or promotion policy during diagnosis.
